Happy Birthday, my Babu. ❤️

Today is your special day, but somehow I feel like I'm the lucky one— because four years ago, I got someone who slowly became such a beautiful part of my life.

Four years... it sounds like just a number, but for me, it carries thousands of little memories.

The laughter, the silly conversations, the random moments, the beautiful days, the difficult days, and even the moments when things weren't perfect.

Somehow, through all of it, you became someone I could never simply call "just another person."

I wish I could be beside you today. I wish I could bring you flowers myself, look at your smile, and say "Happy Birthday, Babu" while you're standing right in front of me.

But distance has its own way of reminding us how much someone means to us.

Maybe we can't always be together. Maybe there are miles between us, different days, different moments, and sometimes life makes things complicated.

But there are some people who remain close to your heart no matter how far away they are.

And you're one of those people for me.

I don't know what the future has written for us, but I genuinely hope that life gives you everything your heart deserves.

I hope you keep smiling, keep chasing your dreams, and never forget how special you are.

And if someday we look back at these four years from a much happier and closer place, I hope we'll smile and say—

"We really went through a lot, but look how far we've come."

So today, forget everything for a while. Enjoy your day. Smile a little more. Take beautiful pictures.

And whenever you look at this message, remember that somewhere, someone is thinking about you and wishing that he could be there to celebrate your day with you.
